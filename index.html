    About

    I trained as an animator, then moved into UX because I wanted to know why people tap where they tap. I spent a year designing e-learning products at Tata Technologies and I'm now completing an M.Des in UI/UX. I work from research to prototype: I test with real users, map the service around the screen, and use motion to explain state changes. I'm looking for a UX design role on a product team where research informs decisions.
